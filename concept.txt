The concept is a personalized, budget-conscious meal-planning software that helps users decide what to eat and exactly what to buy each week. Users select their grocery store, weekly budget, household size, diet, and food preferences, and the platform automatically creates a meal plan that fits those constraints, calculates the required ingredients, consolidates them into a shopping list, and estimates the total cost. The system can also optimize the plan by reusing ingredients across multiple meals, minimizing food waste, suggesting cheaper alternatives, and eventually using real-time supermarket prices to create the cheapest possible grocery basket while still meeting the user’s nutritional and dietary requirements.
